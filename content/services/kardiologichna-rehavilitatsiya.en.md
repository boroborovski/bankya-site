Cardiac rehabilitation is conducted under the supervision of a cardiologist and rehabilitation team. The aim is safe restoration of physical capacity and improved quality of life after a cardiac event.

## Indications

- Myocardial infarction
- Coronary artery bypass and percutaneous coronary interventions
- Chronic heart failure (stabilised)
- Valve surgery

## Treatment Methods

Graded physical activity with ECG monitoring, breathing exercises, healthy lifestyle education, psychological support and balneotherapy.
