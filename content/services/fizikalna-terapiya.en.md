Physical therapy encompasses a wide range of device-based methods using physical factors for treatment. Procedures are performed in well-equipped treatment rooms by medical specialists.

## Methods

- **Electrotherapy** — galvanisation, iontophoresis, diadynamic currents, TENS
- **Ultrasound** — phonophoresis, combined therapy
- **Magnetic therapy** — low-frequency and high-frequency magnetic field
- **Laser therapy** — low-intensity laser for pain and inflammation
- **Paraffin therapy** — thermal treatment for joints and soft tissue
- **Cryotherapy** — cold treatment for acute trauma
