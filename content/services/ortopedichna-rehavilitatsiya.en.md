Orthopaedic rehabilitation is designed for patients with injuries and diseases of the musculoskeletal system. The goal is full restoration of function, strength and range of motion after trauma or surgery.

## Indications

- Total hip and knee arthroplasty
- Limb and spinal fractures
- Tendon and ligament injuries
- Degenerative joint disease
- Post-operative rehabilitation

## Treatment Methods

Individual physiotherapy, mechanotherapy, hydrotherapy in mineral water, physical therapy (ultrasound, magnetic therapy, laser) and manual therapy.
