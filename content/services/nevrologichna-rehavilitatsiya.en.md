Neurological rehabilitation at BPLR–Bankya is designed for patients with impairments of the central and peripheral nervous system. Treatment is delivered by a multidisciplinary team including a neurologist, physiotherapist, occupational therapist and speech therapist.

## Indications

- Cerebral stroke (ischaemic and haemorrhagic)
- Traumatic brain injury
- Parkinson's disease
- Multiple sclerosis
- Peripheral neuropathies
- Post-operative neurological complications

## Treatment Methods

Individual and group physiotherapy, occupational therapy, hydrotherapy in mineral water, physical therapy (electrotherapy, magnetic therapy, laser) and massage.
