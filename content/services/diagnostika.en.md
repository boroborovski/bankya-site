The hospital has a diagnostic facility providing all necessary investigations during the patient's stay, without the need to transfer to another medical centre.

## Diagnostic Capabilities

- 12-lead ECG
- Echocardiography
- Holter ECG and blood pressure monitoring
- Doppler vascular examination
- Spirometry
- Functional exercise capacity tests
- Clinical laboratory (blood tests, urinalysis)

## Consultations

Specialist consultations are available from neurologists, cardiologists, orthopaedic surgeons and other specialists.
