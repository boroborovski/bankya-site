Balneotherapy uses the unique properties of Bankya's mineral water — lightly mineralised, hypotonic, with a temperature of approximately 37°C. The water has a proven therapeutic effect for numerous chronic conditions.

## Types of Procedures

- General and partial mineral baths
- Underwater hydromassage (Scottish douche)
- Underwater gymnastics
- Hydrotherapy
- Whirlpool baths for limbs

## Indications

Musculoskeletal disorders, neurological conditions, skin diseases, gynaecological conditions and rehabilitation after trauma.
