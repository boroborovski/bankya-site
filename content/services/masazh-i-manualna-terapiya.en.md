Massage and manual therapy are an integral part of rehabilitation treatment, delivered by experienced specialists and effective for a wide range of conditions.

## Types of Massage and Manual Therapy

- Classical therapeutic massage (general and local)
- Segmental reflex massage
- Periosteal massage
- Post-isometric muscle relaxation
- Maitland joint mobilisation
- Soft tissue techniques (myofascial release)
