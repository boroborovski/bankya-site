Kinesiotherapy is the core method in rehabilitation treatment. It is delivered by qualified physiotherapists in a modern, well-equipped rehabilitation gym.

## Types of Kinesiotherapy

- Individual therapeutic gymnastics
- Group therapeutic gymnastics
- Mechanotherapy
- Manual therapy and post-isometric relaxation
- Proprioceptive neuromuscular facilitation (PNF)
- Balance training
