Occupational therapy aims to restore the ability to live independently and perform daily activities. It is particularly important for patients after stroke, traumatic brain injury and amputations.

## Areas of Practice

- Assessment of functional abilities
- Training in daily living activities (eating, dressing, personal hygiene)
- Cognitive rehabilitation
- Training with assistive devices (walking frames, canes, orthoses)
- Home environment adaptation
- Work rehabilitation
