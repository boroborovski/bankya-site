BPLR–Bankya has a contract with the National Social Security Institute (NSI) for prophylaxis and rehabilitation services. Eligible insured persons can receive funded treatment at our hospital.

## Who Is Eligible

Employees insured against occupational accidents and occupational diseases under the Social Insurance Code may be eligible for NSI-funded rehabilitation if recommended by the treating physician.

## How to Apply

1. Obtain a referral from your GP or treating specialist confirming the need for rehabilitation.
2. Apply to your local NSI office with the referral and supporting medical documentation.
3. The NSI office issues a voucher for rehabilitation at an approved facility.
4. Contact our admissions office to arrange your admission date using the NSI voucher.

## Required Documents

- NSI voucher
- National ID card
- Medical documentation (discharge summaries, specialist letters)

For further information about NSI eligibility and the application process, please contact your local NSI office or our admissions team.
