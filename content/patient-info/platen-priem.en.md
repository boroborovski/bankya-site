Patients who wish to use our rehabilitation services without a referral or health insurance can be admitted on a private (self-funded) basis.

## What Is Included

Private admission includes accommodation, meals, medical supervision and a personalised rehabilitation programme tailored to your needs and goals.

Procedures and consultations are priced individually depending on the treatment plan agreed with the physician.

## How to Arrange Private Admission

Contact our admissions office by phone or email to discuss your needs, check bed availability and receive a quote. We will be happy to arrange a convenient admission date.

## Benefits of Private Admission

- No referral or insurance required
- Flexible admission dates
- Personalised programme from day one
- Choice of accommodation type (subject to availability)

Please contact us for current pricing and further information.
