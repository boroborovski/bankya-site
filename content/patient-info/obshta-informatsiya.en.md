BPLR–Bankya admits patients for rehabilitation and long-term treatment. Below you will find key information about the admission process, what to bring and what to expect during your stay.

## How to Be Admitted

Admission to the hospital is possible through several routes:

- **NHIF clinical pathways** — for insured patients with a referral from a specialist
- **National Social Security Institute (NSI)** — for patients eligible for prophylaxis and rehabilitation benefits
- **Private admission** — self-funded, without a referral
- **Military personnel** — for active and reserve military members and their families

## What to Bring

- National ID card
- Health insurance card (NHIF)
- Referral from a specialist doctor (GP or specialist)
- Medical documentation: discharge summaries, imaging results, lab tests
- List of current medications

## Working Hours

The admissions office works Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:00. For urgent matters please call us in advance.
