BPLR–Bankya holds a contract with the National Health Insurance Fund (NHIF) and admits patients under four clinical pathways for rehabilitation.

## Available Clinical Pathways

| No. | Pathway | Indication |
|-----|---------|------------|
| 262.1 | Rehabilitation of diseases of the nervous system | Stroke, neurological conditions |
| 263.1 | Rehabilitation of diseases of the musculoskeletal system | Orthopaedic conditions, fractures |
| 264 | Rehabilitation after myocardial infarction | Cardiac rehabilitation |
| 265.1 | Rehabilitation of other diseases | Other indications |

## Requirements for Admission

- Valid health insurance (NHIF)
- Referral (Form 7) from a specialist with the relevant diagnosis
- Medical documentation confirming the indication for the clinical pathway
- National ID card and health insurance card

## Length of Stay

The length of stay is defined by the clinical pathway — typically 10 to 20 days depending on the pathway and the patient's condition.

## How to Apply

Contact our admissions office by phone or in person to check bed availability and arrange an admission date. Please bring all required documents on the day of admission.
