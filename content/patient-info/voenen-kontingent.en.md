As a hospital within the Military Medical Academy (VMA), BPLR–Bankya provides rehabilitation services for military personnel and their families.

## Who Is Eligible

- Active military servicemembers of the Bulgarian Armed Forces
- Reserve military personnel
- Civilian employees of the Ministry of Defence
- Family members of the above categories (subject to bed availability)

## How to Be Admitted

Military personnel are admitted through the Military Medical Academy system. A referral from a VMA physician or the unit medical officer is required.

Please contact our admissions office or coordinate through your unit's medical service for further details on the admission procedure.

## Services Available

All rehabilitation services available to civilian patients are also available to military personnel, including balneotherapy, physiotherapy, neurological and orthopaedic rehabilitation.
